Pick the primary spine

A

By Room → Category  (recommended)

The residential "Selections Schedule" most design-build GCs use. Homeowners navigate by room (matches your app's room-centric model), each line carries the trade/category + pro spec columns. One-click re-group by Trade or CSI, and export a clean schedule for the contractor.

B

By Trade / Discipline Schedules

Separate pro schedules: Plumbing Fixture Schedule, Finish Schedule, Door & Window Schedule, Lighting Schedule, FF&E. Maps to your existing workItemTypes taxonomy. Very "architect," but fragmented for a homeowner browsing one room at a time.

C

By CSI MasterFormat Division

Group by division number — 08 Openings (doors/windows), 09 Finishes, 22 Plumbing, 26 Electrical… The commercial-spec standard. Maximally "official," least intuitive for a homeowner. Best kept as an optional export view layered on top of A.
