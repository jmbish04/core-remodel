Diagram outside, Atelier inside — three compositions

All three share one committed world: room lines in permanent functional color, uniform stops, ringed interchanges, flat ground, no cards. What varies is the structure the world is asked to carry on the Home screen — the one thing a still image can actually settle.

Rooms are 126 Colby's real rooms. All figures, counts and dates are synthetic placeholders. Rendered on the dark ground; light is co-primary and authored equally — the room-line set is checked against both at the bottom.

What it gets right

What it gets right

Dependency is the picture, not a footnote. You can see that the bath was reopened through a shared wall, and that the panel is the next thing at risk. Dashed segments read as "not yet real" without a legend.

What it costs

Readiness is gone — there is no single glance that answers "who can I talk to the trade about." Hand-placed geometry does not auto-layout to twenty rooms, and this is the composition most likely to become decorative.
