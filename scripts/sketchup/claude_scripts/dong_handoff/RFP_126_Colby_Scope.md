## 2. Project intent & basis of need (existing conditions)

This is a **whole-house remodel driven by health, sanitation, and life-safety conditions**, not a discretionary upgrade. The home sustained **repeated storm damage** and we have lived with the consequences for years:

- **All three bathrooms leak.** The upstairs **primary bath sits directly above the lower-level bath** and has leaked into it repeatedly; the **hall bath** has also leaked multiple times due to the failing primary toilet.
- **Sewage has backed up** into the downstairs bathroom; the downstairs toilet and finishes are heavily water-damaged.
- **Roof damage led to interior electrical damage** — wiring was cut/damaged in the primary ceiling (required tracing/repair).
- Ongoing water intrusion continues to worsen finishes and framing.

We would appreciate your guidance on framing the permit application around these **sanitation and life-safety repairs** with DBI, since that is the genuine driver for the bathroom and plumbing work.
